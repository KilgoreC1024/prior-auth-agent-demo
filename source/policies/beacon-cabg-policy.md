# Beacon Insurance — Utilization Management Policy BCN-CABG-120
## Coronary Artery Bypass Graft (CABG)

**Effective date:** March 1, 2026 (synthetic document for coursework — not a real payer policy)

### 1. Scope

This policy applies to prior-authorization requests for elective and urgent
coronary artery bypass graft (CABG) surgery for Beacon Insurance members.

### 2. Clinical Indications

CABG may be authorized when the clinical record documents one of the
following:

- Significant left main coronary artery disease (stenosis ≥ 50%)
- Multivessel coronary artery disease with proximal LAD involvement
- Diabetes mellitus with multivessel disease where surgical
  revascularization is preferred per heart team review
- Refractory angina despite maximal medical therapy with anatomy suitable
  for grafting

### 3. Required Documentation

1. **Cardiothoracic surgery consultation note** with operative plan
2. **Coronary angiography report** demonstrating qualifying anatomy
3. **Recent echocardiogram or ventriculogram** documenting LVEF
4. **Documentation of guideline-directed medical therapy** trialed prior to
   surgery, where applicable

### 4. Authorization Period

Approved authorizations are valid for 120 calendar days.

### 5. Review Standard

Incomplete submissions will be pended for 10 business days before an
administrative determination is made.
