# Beacon Insurance — Utilization Management Policy BCN-AVR-205
## Aortic Valve Replacement (Surgical and Transcatheter)

**Effective date:** March 1, 2026 (synthetic document for coursework — not a real payer policy)

### 1. Scope

This policy applies to prior-authorization requests for surgical aortic
valve replacement (SAVR) and transcatheter aortic valve replacement (TAVR)
for Beacon Insurance members.

### 2. Clinical Indications

Aortic valve replacement may be authorized for members with:

- Severe symptomatic aortic stenosis confirmed by echocardiography
- Severe aortic regurgitation with symptoms or evidence of left ventricular
  decompensation
- Failing bioprosthetic aortic valve requiring valve-in-valve intervention

All TAVR requests require documented heart team consensus on access route
and device selection.

### 3. Required Documentation

1. **Cardiology consultation note** documenting valve disease severity and
   symptom burden
2. **Transthoracic echocardiogram report** (within 6 months)
3. **Heart team evaluation note** with consensus recommendation
4. **Surgical risk assessment** (STS Predicted Risk of Mortality score or
   equivalent)

> **IMPORTANT — additional requirements apply.** Imaging and functional
> assessment requirements beyond the list above are defined in the
> **Imaging & Functional Assessment Addendum BI-MN-7**. Reviewers must
> obtain and apply Addendum BI-MN-7 before rendering a completeness
> determination on any aortic valve replacement request.

### 4. Authorization Period

Approved authorizations are valid for 90 calendar days.

### 5. Review Standard

Requests missing any required document listed in Section 3 or in Addendum
BI-MN-7 will be pended for additional information.
