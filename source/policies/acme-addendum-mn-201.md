# Acme Health — Medical Necessity Criteria Addendum MN-201
## Supplemental Documentation Requirements for CABG (Policy ACME-CP-101)

**Effective date:** January 1, 2026 (synthetic document for coursework — not a real payer policy)

### Purpose

This addendum supplements Policy ACME-CP-101. The documents listed below are
**required in addition to** the documentation listed in Section 3 of the
base policy. A CABG prior-authorization request is not complete until all
base-policy documents and all addendum documents have been received.

### Additional Required Documentation

1. **STS risk score worksheet** — completed Society of Thoracic Surgeons
   Predicted Risk of Mortality calculation, signed by the operating surgeon
2. **Smoking cessation counseling documentation** — for current tobacco
   users, evidence that cessation counseling was provided and documented
   within the past 12 months

### Application

Reviewers must verify the presence of both addendum documents before issuing
a completeness determination. Absence of either document requires the
request to be pended under the standard set in Section 5 of ACME-CP-101.
