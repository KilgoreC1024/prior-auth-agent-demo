# Acme Health — Prior Authorization Policy ACME-CP-101
## Coronary Artery Bypass Graft (CABG)

**Effective date:** January 1, 2026 (synthetic document for coursework — not a real payer policy)

### 1. Scope

This policy governs prior-authorization requests for coronary artery bypass
graft (CABG) surgery, including on-pump and off-pump procedures, submitted on
behalf of Acme Health commercial and Medicare Advantage members.

### 2. Clinical Indications

CABG may be considered medically necessary when at least one of the following
is documented:

- Left main coronary artery stenosis of 50% or greater
- Three-vessel coronary artery disease with stenosis of 70% or greater
- Two-vessel disease involving the proximal left anterior descending (LAD)
  artery with reduced left ventricular function (LVEF below 50%)
- Failed or unsuitable anatomy for percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI),
  as documented by the treating interventional cardiologist

### 3. Required Documentation

The following documents must accompany every CABG prior-authorization request:

1. **Cardiology consultation note** describing symptoms, functional status,
   and the indication for surgical revascularization
2. **Coronary angiography report** demonstrating the qualifying anatomy
3. **Echocardiogram report** with left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF)
   measured within the past 90 days
4. **Heart team conference note** documenting multidisciplinary review of
   surgical versus percutaneous options

> **IMPORTANT — additional requirements apply.** Medical-necessity
> documentation requirements beyond the list above are defined in the
> **Medical Necessity Criteria Addendum MN-201**. Reviewers must obtain and
> apply Addendum MN-201 before rendering a completeness determination on any
> CABG request.

### 4. Authorization Period

Approved authorizations are valid for 90 calendar days from the date of
determination. Expedited review is available when the treating physician
certifies that the standard timeframe could seriously jeopardize the
member's health.

### 5. Review Standard

Requests missing any required document listed in Section 3 or in Addendum
MN-201 will be pended for additional information and may be administratively
denied if the documentation is not received within 14 calendar days.
