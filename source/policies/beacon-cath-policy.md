# Beacon Insurance — Utilization Management Policy BCN-CATH-310
## Diagnostic Cardiac Catheterization

**Effective date:** March 1, 2026 (synthetic document for coursework — not a real payer policy)

### 1. Scope

This policy applies to prior-authorization requests for elective diagnostic
cardiac catheterization (left heart catheterization with or without coronary
angiography) for Beacon Insurance members. Emergency procedures are exempt
from prior authorization.

### 2. Clinical Indications

Diagnostic cardiac catheterization may be authorized when the record
documents one of the following:

- Abnormal noninvasive stress test (exercise ECG, stress echocardiography,
  or nuclear perfusion imaging)
- Angina symptoms with intermediate-to-high pretest probability of
  obstructive coronary artery disease
- Evaluation prior to planned cardiac surgery
- Suspected cardiomyopathy or unexplained heart failure requiring
  hemodynamic assessment

### 3. Required Documentation

1. **Referring physician order** for the catheterization
2. **Clinical notes documenting symptoms** (angina, dyspnea, or equivalent)
   or the abnormal finding prompting the study
3. **Noninvasive stress test results** or equivalent cardiac imaging
4. **Current medication list**

### 4. Authorization Period

Approved authorizations are valid for 60 calendar days.

### 5. Review Standard

Incomplete submissions will be pended for 10 business days before an
administrative determination is made.
