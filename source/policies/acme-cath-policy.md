# Acme Health — Prior Authorization Policy ACME-CP-115
## Diagnostic Cardiac Catheterization

**Effective date:** January 1, 2026 (synthetic document for coursework — not a real payer policy)

### 1. Scope

This policy governs prior-authorization requests for elective diagnostic
left heart catheterization and coronary angiography for Acme Health members.
Emergent and urgent inpatient catheterizations do not require prior
authorization.

### 2. Clinical Indications

Diagnostic cardiac catheterization may be considered medically necessary
when at least one of the following is documented:

- Abnormal or inconclusive noninvasive stress testing with intermediate-to-high
  pretest probability of obstructive coronary artery disease
- Stable angina not adequately controlled on guideline-directed medical therapy
- Pre-operative evaluation before planned valve surgery or other cardiac surgery
- New unexplained reduction in left ventricular function

### 3. Required Documentation

1. **Physician referral or order** for the catheterization, signed and dated
2. **Clinical notes documenting ischemic symptoms** or the abnormal finding
   prompting the study
3. **Prior noninvasive stress test or cardiac imaging results**
4. **Current medication list**, including review of antiplatelet and
   anticoagulant therapy

### 4. Authorization Period

Approved authorizations are valid for 60 calendar days from the date of
determination.

### 5. Review Standard

Requests missing any required document listed in Section 3 will be pended
for additional information.
