# Acme Health — Prior Authorization Policy ACME-CP-108
## Aortic Valve Replacement (Surgical and Transcatheter)

**Effective date:** January 1, 2026 (synthetic document for coursework — not a real payer policy)

### 1. Scope

This policy governs prior-authorization requests for surgical aortic valve
replacement (SAVR) and transcatheter aortic valve replacement (TAVR) for
Acme Health members.

### 2. Clinical Indications

Aortic valve replacement may be considered medically necessary for:

- Severe symptomatic aortic stenosis (aortic valve area ≤ 1.0 cm², mean
  gradient ≥ 40 mmHg, or peak velocity ≥ 4.0 m/s)
- Severe aortic regurgitation with symptoms or LVEF below 55%
- Asymptomatic severe aortic stenosis with LVEF below 50%

TAVR specifically requires heart team concurrence that transcatheter access
is appropriate given the member's surgical risk profile.

### 3. Required Documentation

1. **Cardiology consultation note** documenting symptoms and valve disease
   severity
2. **Transthoracic echocardiogram report** quantifying the valve lesion
   (within 6 months)
3. **Heart team evaluation note** (required for all TAVR requests;
   recommended for SAVR)
4. **Surgical risk assessment** (STS Predicted Risk of Mortality score or
   equivalent)

### 4. Authorization Period

Approved authorizations are valid for 90 calendar days from the date of
determination.

### 5. Review Standard

Requests missing any required document listed in Section 3 will be pended
for additional information.
